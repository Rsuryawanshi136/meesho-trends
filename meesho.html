          How this report works
          • Every day at 9 PM, the first page of two public Meesho listings per category is read, in Meesho's default order. Nothing behind a login is used.
          • Meesho doesn't publish sales. All-time ranks by total reviews. Today ranks by reviews gained since the previous day, which tracks daily sales.
