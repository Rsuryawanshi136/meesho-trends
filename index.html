About BuyerMind

Your Research, Done Every Day

What is this?

A daily report on what's selling on Indian online marketplaces. Each day, we read public product listings, compare them with the last update, and turn the numbers into a short, simple summary.

  • Top sellers in 10 categories
  • Today's fastest movers and all-time best sellers
  • Prices, ratings and complaint levels

How does it help?

Choosing the wrong product is an expensive way to learn. BuyerMind gives you evidence to look at before you spend money on stock.

  • Spot products that are gaining buyers
  • Avoid categories with many complaints
  • See the typical price in each category
  • Plan ahead for festivals and seasons

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants to understand what Indian shoppers are buying, especially if you're selling or about to start.

  • New sellers choosing a first product
  • Existing sellers looking for what to add next
  • Resellers and small shops deciding what to stock
  • Researchers and creators following online trends

How It Works

From Public Listings to Plain Answers

  1. 1. Read

    Each day, public listing pages are read, the same ones any shopper can see. Nothing behind a login is used.

  2. 2. Compare

    Today's numbers are compared with the previous update, so you see what is moving and not just what is big.

  3. 3. Explain

    Each category gets a short read: what's selling, what's rising, and how suitable it is for a beginner.

These numbers are estimates. Marketplaces don't publish sales, so reviews and rankings stand in for them. Use them to compare products, not as exact order counts.
